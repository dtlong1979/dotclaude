Đích Q1: **Journal of Pragmatics** (framing #1, AI xuống Methods); dự phòng Languages in Contrast (Q2), Corpus Pragmatics (Q2, mê AI). Deliverable: `Project1\D1_CHOT_huong_nghien_cuu.md`.
